What it does

Hover, don't guess

Mouse over an item and a little box tells you if it's a steal, fair, or a rip-off, going off what it's actually sold for. Not what someone listed it at.

Just shop like normal

Open shops and scroll like you always do. It reads the rows for you. No typing item names into a site with your character standing there AFK.

Everyone's browsing counts

Every shop any of us opens goes into the same price history. More of us in the FM means fresher prices for all of us.

Won't make stuff up

If it's only seen one sale, it tells you that. It'd rather say "not sure" than talk you into a bad buy.

Pricing your own shop

Setting up your shop? It shows what each item moves fast at, what's normal, and what you can get if you're patient. Stop underpricing your 60%s.

Won't get you banned

It only looks at your screen, like OBS does. It never touches the game, never reads its memory, never clicks anything for you.

Why we made it

Everyone's been there: you buy something for 500k, walk two rooms over and it's sitting at 200k. The only way to know prices is to camp the FM for days and remember everything. Price sites help a bit, but they only update when someone happens to open shops on stream, so half the time they're stale or missing. We're clicking through shops all day anyway, so this just remembers it for us and shares it with the group.

Getting started

  1. Message otis on Discord for an invite key.
  2. Download and run MesoMeter-win-Setup.exe. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed yet: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Meso Meter starts in the system tray (the orange M near the clock). Paste your invite key in the Settings window and save.
  4. Go shopping. Prices pop up when you hover stuff. It updates itself, so you never have to redownload.

Heads up, it's a beta. It's just a few of us on Kania for now, so plenty of items will say "No sales seen yet" until we've all browsed around a bit. If a price looks way off, ping otis. That's the stuff we most want to know about.

Windows only. Play MapleStory Classic World windowed at 1366×768; other sizes aren't read yet.
